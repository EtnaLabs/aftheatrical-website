AF Theatricals is an experiential production company creating spaces where ideas, culture, and community converge. From intimate salons to immersive gatherings, we produce experiences that bring people together — because every gathering is a production.

Our Philosophy

The Art of Gathering

We believe the best ideas emerge when people come together with intention. Whether exploring the frontiers of AI or celebrating the power of community, each AF Theatricals event is designed to be a moment you carry with you long after the lights go down.

From curated social gatherings to intellectually charged meetups, we design moments that spark connection and leave a lasting impression. We curate the setting, the conversation, and the energy — so all you have to do is show up.

“Every gathering is an act of creation.”
AF Theatricals

01 — Curated Salons

The Women's Salon

An intimate salon draped in velvet, where guests connect over shared passions, great conversation, and the warmth of community. The Women's Salon is our curated salon series — a thoughtfully designed setting where every detail, from the table to the atmosphere, is produced with care.

Like all of our curated salons, it is built around a simple idea: gatherings become memorable when curation, connection, atmosphere, and celebration come together in one room.

02 — AI-Powered Cultural Gatherings

AI Socratic Gatherings

A loft buzzing with ideas about artificial intelligence. Our AI Socratic gatherings bring Socratic-style discussion to the frontiers of AI — intellectually charged meetups where ideas, culture, and community converge.

These are conversations produced like performances: the setting, the questions, and the energy are all curated so that the best ideas can emerge when people come together with intention.
