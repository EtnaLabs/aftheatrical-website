Who we are

AF Theatricals is a New York experiential production company led by Anissa Felix, a Broadway and TV actor turned creative event designer. Having directed and produced plays, films, and live performances, we bring a director's instincts to event curation.

What we do

We bring everything we know from the theatre into one cohesive "salon" — a single theme, fully curated, from the food to the cocktails to the moderated conversation. Our audiences leave with an experience that stays with them.

What to expect

We work with a deep bench of creative artists to bring your event together. High-impact storytelling, elegant design, and a perspective you won't find anywhere else.

Approach

Storytelling

This is the root of all our work. Every gathering is centered on a narrative with a clear beginning, middle, and end. The conversation begins the moment guests enter the venue — and when the storytelling is strong, they carry it with them long after the event has ended.

Exciting artistic partnerships

We collaborate with NYFW photographers, Broadway vocalists, musicians, experimental mixologists, and up-and-coming artists. We love hiring the people working at the frontier of their field.

It's all in the details

We conduct deep research to make the small touches land hard. Your guests will be surprised when they spot a cleverly placed detail in a setting they hadn't expected.

Services

Event Programming

From the spark of inspiration, we translate your idea into a fully produced salon. We keep it intimate and highly personalized, and we keep your product at the center of the conversation.

Event Design

We create the biggest impact, affordably. With a clever use of lighting, florals, and hyper-personalized details, we take a beautiful event to undeniable.

Audience Outreach

We help build the guest list from a wide-ranging social network, conducting deep research to find the right people — often contacts clients hadn't considered, who bring incredible value to the room.

Hosting

Not shy at all. With Broadway and TV experience, we emcee the event, make every guest feel special, and keep the run-of-show flowing — buffering any social interaction with style and grace.
