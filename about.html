About AF Theatricals

Where Community Takes Center Stage

AF Theatricals is an experiential production company based in New York City. We produce immersive experiences that bring people together — from intimate salons to theater performances — because every gathering is a production.

Who We Are

Every Gathering Is a Performance

AF Theatricals creates spaces where ideas, culture, and community converge. From curated social gatherings to intellectually charged meetups, we design moments that spark connection and leave a lasting impression.

Whether it’s an intimate salon draped in velvet or a loft buzzing with ideas about artificial intelligence, every AF Theatricals event is thoughtfully produced. We curate the setting, the conversation, and the energy — so all you have to do is show up.

“Every gathering is an act of creation.”
AF Theatricals

We believe the best ideas emerge when people come together with intention. Whether exploring the frontiers of AI or celebrating the power of community, each event is designed to be a moment you carry with you long after the lights go down.
